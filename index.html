Gathered every morning from hens that roam the yard. Brown, blue and speckled, whatever they feel like giving us.

How It Works

The honor system, in three easy steps

No cashier, no line, no hurry. The stand is open whether we are out there or not — we trust you, and folks have never given us a reason not to.

1

Stop by any day, 9 to 7

Seven days a week, rain or shine. Pull in at the stand on County Road 2200 N and take your time.

2

Pick out your goods

Eggs in the cooler, honey and gifts on the shelves, produce and flowers out front. Prices are marked on everything.

3

Pay how it suits you

Leave cash in the box, or send it over from your phone before you pull away. That is the whole checkout.

Our Story

A small farm run by the women of one family

Slifer Farm sits on a quiet stretch of County Road 2200 N just outside Champaign. It is a working family farm, run by the women in the family, and the stand out front is how we share what it produces.

The hens roam the yard and lay where they please, so the egg cartons come out speckled and mismatched in the best way. The honey comes from our own hives, only a few hundred feet from the flowers the bees work all summer. Cookies get baked in the farmhouse kitchen most mornings, and whatever the garden hands over that week goes straight out to the table.

There is no register and no one standing behind the counter. Just a marked price, a cash box, and the assumption that our neighbors will do the right thing. Forty-four reviews later, they always have.

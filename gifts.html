Handmade Gifts

Handmade Gifts from the Farm

A shelf of small things made and grown here, tucked inside the stand and out of the weather. Earrings, luffa soaps, framed prints, greeting cards, vanilla extract and honey, all on the same honor system as the eggs.

Open Daily 9 AM – 7 PM Made right here on the farm
On the Gift Shelf

Made here, sold here

Nothing on this shelf came off a truck. It is made or grown on the farm, priced on the label, and paid for in the box or from your phone.

Handmade Earrings

Earrings made by hand here on the farm and set out on the shelf as they are finished. What is there changes as pairs sell, so it is worth a proper look each time you stop.

  • Made by hand
  • One of a kind
  • Easy to mail

Framed Photo Prints

Photographs of the farm and the country around it, printed and framed ready to hang. Fields, flowers, hens and the seasons as they turn.

  • Farm & nature photography
  • Framed
  • Ready to hang

Luffa Soaps

The luffa is grown in our own patch, dried out and set into the bar, so the sponge and the soap are one piece. They scrub properly and they last a good while.

  • Grown on the farm
  • Year-round
  • Winter staple

Photo Greeting Cards

Farm photographs made up as greeting cards, ready for birthdays, thank yous and the notes that are much nicer to mail than to text. They stay on the shelf all winter.

  • Farm photographs
  • Winter staple
  • Easy to mail

Homemade Vanilla Extract

Made here in the farmhouse in small batches and bottled for the shelf. A quiet favorite with anyone who bakes, and it keeps happily in a cupboard until they need it.

  • Homemade
  • Small batch
  • Year-round

Raw Honey as a Gift

A jar of honey from our own hives suits very nearly everybody, travels well and needs no explaining. It is on the shelf every month of the year.

  • Our own hives
  • Year-round
  • Travels well

Gifts by Occasion

Something for whoever you have in mind

If you are standing at the shelf and not sure where to start, here is what tends to suit what.

Stocking Stuffers

Small, flat and easy to wrap. A pair of earrings, a bar of luffa soap or a bottle of vanilla extract fills a stocking without any fuss at all.

  • Earrings
  • Luffa soap
  • Vanilla extract

Hostess & Teacher Gifts

For the neighbor who had you over for dinner, or the teacher you keep meaning to thank. A jar of honey with a photo card tucked alongside says it plainly.

  • Raw honey
  • Greeting cards
  • Luffa soap

Wedding & Shower Favors

Small, simple and made by hand, which is what a favor wants to be. Luffa soaps and little jars of honey both look lovely set out in a row. If you need several, call first and we can tell you what is on the shelf.

  • Luffa soaps
  • Raw honey
  • Call ahead

Treat Yourself

No occasion needed, and nobody is watching. A framed print for the hallway, a bar of soap for your own bathroom and a jar of honey for the cupboard.

  • Framed prints
  • Luffa soap
  • Raw honey

This is the shelf that carries the stand through winter. When the produce table rests, the honey, luffa soaps, photo greeting cards and vanilla extract stay exactly where they are, and the eggs keep coming as long as the hens are willing. Most of it is made by hand and one of a kind, so the shelf changes as things sell; if you see something you like, it is worth taking it there and then.

Come and Have a Look

The gift shelf is open every day

It sits inside the stand, out of the weather, from 9 in the morning until 7 at night. Take what you like and leave your payment in the box, or send it over from your phone.
